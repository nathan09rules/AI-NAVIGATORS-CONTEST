SYMPTOMS

List of all symptoms and their descriptions

Phlegm

Phlegm is the thick, sticky mucus that your body makes, especially when you're sick. It comes from your lungs and throat and is usually clear but can turn yellow, green, or brown if you have an infection.

Stomach Pain

Stomach pain is any discomfort or ache felt in the belly area. It can be mild or severe, and may come and go. Common causes include indigestion, gas, infections, or cramps.

Nausea

Nausea is the feeling that you might throw up, like your stomach is upset or uneasy. It can happen due to spoiled food, motion sickness, infections, or stress.

Burning Micturition

Burning micturition means feeling a burning or painful sensation while urinating.

Diarrhoea

Diarrhea is when you have loose or watery stools more often than usual.
